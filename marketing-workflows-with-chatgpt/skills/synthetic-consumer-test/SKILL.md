# Synthetic Consumer Test

## Inputs
Drafts, decision being explored, and relevant real customer language with source context and permission to use it. There is no magic minimum number of quotes that makes a simulation representative.

## Workflow
If evidence is absent, request it or, with the user's acceptance, offer an explicitly unsupported critique. Use source-based perspectives and include disagreement. Distinguish actual quotations from generated reactions. Look for confusion, missing information, and potential barriers.

Do not produce population percentages, purchase probability, demand forecasts, or validated rankings from simulated personas. More simulated respondents do not create a representative sample. Do not numerically correct supposed model bias by an invented factor.

## Output and checks
Label every result simulated. Return source-grounded questions, disagreements, evidence gaps, and a real-person research plan. Use actual target buyers to test comprehension, suitability, and purchase barriers before commercial decisions. Do not call this a focus group or a digital twin validation.
