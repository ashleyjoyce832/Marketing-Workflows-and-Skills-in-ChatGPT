# Handmade Proof Content

## Inputs
Actual footage/photos, maker notes, product facts, permissions, intended audience and channel. Concept images may guide shot planning but are not evidence of manufacture.

## Workflow
Identify genuine details that show the making: tools, sequence, materials, scale, decisions, and imperfections. Draft a short shot list, captions, and edit sequence grounded in available evidence. Suggest missing shots as a capture plan, not footage that exists.

## Output and checks
Deliver a practical capture/edit plan with source asset IDs and factual caption checks. Keep the maker's voice and avoid staging a false process claim. Route unsupported material, sustainability, origin, or performance claims to the product owner. Publishing follows the authorized scope.
