# Proposed launch and learning plan

This plan applies only if the collaboration and product proceed. All dates, budgets, rates, and sales targets require owner decisions. The initial hotel pilot and later consumer launch have separate goals.

## Phase 1: hotel pilot

Before commitment, validate the hotel's objective, decision-maker, artist participation, scope, rights, staffing, guest experience, and cost. Establish how qualified booking inquiries or bookings will be recognized and which data the hotel can share. Keep prospective hotel-partner inquiries separate from guest demand. Use the pilot's documented commercial and qualitative outcomes to decide whether to pitch additional collaborations. Social attention alone is insufficient.

Skills: campaign-idea-pressure-test, audience-evidence-synthesis, campaign-concept-expander, launch-readiness-review.

## Phase 2: consumer readiness

Confirm manufacturing and reproduction arrangements, price, sizes, finish, installation requirements, sampling, stock, fulfillment, refunds, and approved product claims. Conduct real conversations with potential buyers and designers. Use synthetic critique only to generate questions. Select a primary conversion such as a completed sample request when that offer exists, then track full orders separately.

Skills: synthetic-consumer-test, handmade-proof-content, ad-variation-generator, launch-readiness-review.

## Phase 3: controlled creative learning

Hypothesis: a detail-led curiosity message may increase completed sample requests compared with a room-led benefit message. For a copy-only test, hold the image, offer, eligible audience, placement, and landing page constant. Use a platform randomization mechanism if available. Do not label sequential organic posts a randomized A/B test.

Record the primary outcome, baseline, minimum meaningful effect, sample-size method, time window, budget cap, allocation, guardrails, and stopping rule before launch. Estimate feasibility from actual traffic. If volume is too low, report directional observations and conduct real qualitative research rather than declaring a winner.

Skills: experiment-plan, channel-tracking-setup.

## Phase 4: channel operations

| Channel | Job | Record | Owner decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artist organic social | Show art and actual making | Asset ID, format, date, link, qualified visits | Voice, authenticity, posting mix |
| Paid social | Test an approved proposition | Variant, spend, allocation, conversions | Budget and targeting |
| Hotel channels | Explain guest experience | Partner link, inquiries, confirmed bookings | Partner copy and commitments |
| Website | Explain product and fulfill the promise | Sessions, sample and purchase events, errors | Product truth and experience |
| Opted-in email | Early access, sample follow-up, care | Segment, sends, clicks, orders, unsubscribes | Consent, message, cadence |
| Editorial collaboration | Reach a relevant interiors audience | Confirmed placement and referral link | Agreement and disclosure |
| Times Square concept | Potential reach and reusable real footage | Placement details, unique URL/QR, capture plan | Cost and measurement feasibility |

Daily: check links, product availability, fulfillment issues, and customer questions. Weekly: reconcile performance records, review creative mix, and choose one useful next question. After sampling: answer common installation or suitability questions with verified information. After purchase: provide approved care guidance and request feedback where appropriate. This can feed future content and audience evidence.

Skills: channel-operations, audience-evidence-synthesis, performance-digest.

## Phase 5: measurement and decisions

- **Operational diagnosis:** compare channel and creative response using consistent denominators and attribution windows. Do not sum conversions reported independently by different ad platforms.
- **Commercial result:** use deduplicated commerce/booking records, net of refunds and cancellations. Revenue and margin are distinct. Keep samples and final product orders separate.
- **Incremental effect:** tagged links and self-reported discovery help describe observed response. They cannot establish how many outcomes would have occurred without the campaign. Consider a feasible randomized holdout, matched geographic design, or specialist evaluation when spend and sample support it. A single billboard without a credible comparison does not yield causal lift.
- **Workflow value:** compare similar tasks on preparation time, human review time, rework, source accuracy, and accepted output quality. This project did not collect that baseline.

Every readout ends with: what is observed, what remains uncertain, what should change, who owns the decision, and when to check again. No numerical success thresholds are invented in this kit.

Framework sources: OpenAI Academy marketing guide; Google Modern Measurement Playbook, especially attribution and incrementality sections. The concrete campaign plan is a proposed application of those ideas.
