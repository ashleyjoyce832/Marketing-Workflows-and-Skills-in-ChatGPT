# Performance Digest

## Inputs
Results, metric definitions, campaign/test design, relevant context, windows, and source systems. Label simulated data before analysis. If the business question or denominator is unclear, resolve it before comparing rates.

## Workflow
Check missing values, duplicates, overlap, timing, currency, and attribution scope before ranking. Recalculate metrics with explicit denominators. Do not average row-level rates when a ratio of totals is required. Treat zero denominators as undefined.

Report observed differences with counts and uncertainty appropriate to the design. Separate interpretations from evidence. Avoid inventing three drivers or three experiments merely to fill a format. No winner is required when evidence is weak. Attributed revenue is not profit or incremental revenue.

## Output and checks
Return observed facts, data-quality limitations, plausible explanations labeled hypotheses, next useful test, and decisions for the owner. Link every numerical claim to its input/calculation. Do not recommend automatic budget changes based on low counts or inconsistent windows.
