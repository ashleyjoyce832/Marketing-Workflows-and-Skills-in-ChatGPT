# Channel Tracking Setup

## Inputs
Primary outcome, destination, supported analytics/commerce tools, channels, creative IDs, time window, and data access. Verify capabilities rather than assuming free short links or conversion tracking are available.

## Workflow
Define source, medium, campaign, and content naming rules. Give channels and variants distinct tagged links when supported. For offline response, propose a memorable URL/QR and optional self-reported discovery question. Match event definitions to completed actions, not just page views.

Specify deduplication, time zones, attribution windows, and source-of-truth records. Test destination and event behavior before launch. Keep unknown reach/data blank. Flag link stripping and tracking loss.

## Output and checks
Provide a link specification, event dictionary, channel-log schema, and QA checklist. Do not call channels randomized test groups. Organic audiences and exposures differ. Tracking and self-report describe response, not incremental causal lift. Do not create external accounts or modify analytics without authorization.
