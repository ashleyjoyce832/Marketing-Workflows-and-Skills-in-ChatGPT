# A small-business starting point

Choose a task that happens repeatedly: a weekly social draft, event invitation, customer-question summary, or campaign report. Use an offer the business can actually fulfill.

1. Write the goal, intended buyer, offer, confirmed facts, and one desired action in `templates/campaign-brief.md`.
2. Supply real examples of the brand's content and customer language. Explain what to preserve and what feels wrong.
3. Pick one skill. Ask for a complete draft, then judge it against the task's purpose.
4. Record the correction and the reason in `templates/decision-log.csv`. Avoid saving every personal preference as a universal rule.
5. Distill repeated useful corrections into the skill and try a second brief.
6. Before publishing, confirm claims, product availability, links, owner, and authorization. Start with the simplest tracking your platform supports.
7. Compare elapsed preparation time, human review time, rework, and factual errors over repeated similar tasks. More prompts or files do not establish value.

For the wallpaper example, art inspiration can earn attention, but installation, dimensions, finish, delivery, and samples may determine purchase. Obtain those facts from the real product owner. For a local class or event, replace wallpaper orders with completed RSVPs and attendance. The same workflow transfers without needing a billboard or luxury hotel.
