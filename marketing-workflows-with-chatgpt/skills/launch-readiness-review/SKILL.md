# Launch Readiness Review

## Inputs
Concrete assets, approved brief, product facts, live destination if available, fulfillment plan, tracking, and owner approvals. Review the actual package rather than asking approval of a vague plan.

## Workflow
Compare claims to sources and check that the offer can be fulfilled. Distinguish concept render, photographed product, real process footage, confirmed partner, and prospective placement. Identify material blockers, with evidence and responsible owners. Verify links and event setup where access permits.

## Output and checks
Return launch-ready items, fixable issues, unresolved blockers, and the owner decision required. Scope checks to this campaign. Do not certify legal compliance, invent approval, or call a proposed collaborator confirmed. Readiness review does not itself authorize publication.
