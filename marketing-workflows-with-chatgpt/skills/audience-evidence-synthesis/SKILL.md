# Audience Evidence Synthesis

## Inputs
Reviews, interviews, support questions, survey answers, or sales notes with dates and source context. Use deidentified material appropriate to the task.

## Workflow
Separate buyer evidence from followers, guests, and general category comments. Group recurring needs and barriers while retaining contradictory views. Attach source IDs to each theme. Preserve quotations accurately and avoid claims about prevalence beyond the sample.

For wallpaper, distinguish inspiration from suitability, installation, price, sampling, and fulfillment questions. These are inquiry categories, not findings until evidence supports them.

## Output and checks
Return a theme/evidence table, limitations, open research questions, and implications for the current brief. Do not invent interviews, infer protected traits, or treat the loudest comments as representative. Feed validated objections into truthful content or product decisions.
