# Ad Variation Generator

## Inputs
Approved concept, target action, audience, channels, confirmed facts, and reference assets. Inspect the references and identify what to preserve: content mix, image scale, voice, process, or format. Distinguish original work from inspiration.

## Workflow
Build useful alternative messages or executions, with IDs and a reason for each. Adapt to the channel rather than pasting identical ads everywhere. Native artist content may include standalone art and process; it need not have a headline overlay.

For a copy test, keep the visual and offer constant. For a visual test, keep copy and other relevant conditions constant. A creative exploration changing several elements is not a causal test. Do not invent product details, partnership status, customer response, or engagement counts. Use [confirm: fact] for missing publishable facts.

## Output and checks
Return variant IDs, intended channel/format, copy or visual brief, CTA, facts to confirm, and a test hypothesis. Generate assets when authorized and supported. Label hypothetical campaign images. Generated making-process scenes cannot serve as proof of real handmade work.
