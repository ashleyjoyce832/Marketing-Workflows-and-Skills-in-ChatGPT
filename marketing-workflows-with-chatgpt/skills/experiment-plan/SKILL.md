# Experiment Plan

## Inputs
Decision, outcome, variants, traffic, baseline, minimum useful effect, budget, platform controls, and timing. Ask for consequential missing quantities; do not invent them.

## Workflow
State the hypothesis and single intended difference. Define the allocation unit, eligibility, assignment mechanism, overlap controls, primary metric/denominator, and guardrails. Hold other relevant conditions fixed where feasible. Check whether available volume can answer the question.

Prespecify a justified sample-size method, duration, and stopping/analysis rule. Distinguish a fixed-horizon design from a valid sequential method. Do not endorse repeated unplanned peeking. For small observational pilots, provide a descriptive or qualitative plan with explicit limits.

## Output and checks
Deliver a preregistered-style plan with assumptions, feasibility, tracking requirements, decision thresholds supplied or justified, and an inconclusive outcome path. A click increase need not establish a purchase increase. Synthetic personas cannot replace the experiment.
