# Campaign Concept Expander

## Inputs
The marketer's idea, brief, current decisions, evidence, and references. One idea is sufficient. If the idea is missing, ask for it or clarify whether the user wants ideation instead.

## Workflow
Restate the concept and what must remain. Develop a small useful range of expressions, explaining the link to the originating idea. Separate changes in strategic proposition from changes in execution. Carry forward researched constraints and the correct launch phase.

Flag generic elements only when present and show a concrete improvement grounded in the brief. Do not manufacture a criticism to fill a quota. Do not revive rejected directions. Where feasibility is uncertain, label it.

## Output and checks
Provide reviewable directions, why each belongs to the idea, material assumptions, and the next decision. Leave creative selection to the user unless already delegated. Do not require an arbitrary number of concepts or independent approvals for routine drafting.
