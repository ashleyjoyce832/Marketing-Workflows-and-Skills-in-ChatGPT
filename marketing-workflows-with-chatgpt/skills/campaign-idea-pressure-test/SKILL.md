# Campaign Idea Pressure Test

## Inputs
Existing idea, project purpose, intended buyer, desired action, constraints, and known evidence. Reuse answered questions and the latest decisions.

## Workflow
Clarify the teaching/project goal separately from the business outcome. Ask open questions about the intended buyer and the connection between the experience and the action. In the user's ideation mode, preserve authorship and do not supply replacement campaigns. If the user explicitly requests ideas, honor that changed scope.

Research only material audience, brand, or competitor assumptions using relevant sources. Distinguish evidence, inference, and the user's choice. Do not equate income with intent to buy or attention with sales. Explain contradictions and let the user revise or defend the idea. Avoid restarting a completed intake.

Stop when purpose, buyer, action, mechanism, and consequential constraints are clear enough to expand. Defer logistics that will not affect the next decision.

## Output and checks
Return a concise draft brief, sources, assumptions, rejected directions, and unresolved decisions. Preserve the original concept and the current campaign phase. Never invent partner commitment or a quantitative target.
